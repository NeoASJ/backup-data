# Local Document Q&A (DocQuery)

A privacy-focused application for querying sensitive documents completely offline, ensuring no data ever leaves your device or is transmitted over the internet.

---

## Core Capabilities & Features

* **100% Offline & Private:** Process documents locally on your machine with zero cloud connectivity or risk of external data exposure.
* **Universal File Support:** Ingests PDF, plain text (`.txt`), and Markdown (`.md`) files directly into a local knowledge base.
* **Instant Smart Suggestions:** Automatically analyzes uploaded files to generate relevant, clickable question recommendations so you can start exploring immediately.
* **Clean Single-Page Interface:** Features drag-and-drop file loading, interactive topic chips, and real-time processing feedback.
---

## Use Cases

* **Legal & Contract Review:** Analyze confidential agreements, NDAs, and legal filings locally without violating client confidentiality or data privacy regulations.
* **Proprietary Business Intelligence:** Interrogate internal financial reports, company SOPs, strategy docs, and customer data safely offline.
* **Personal & Sensitive Records:** Securely query personal medical records, tax filings, and private notes without uploading them to public cloud services.
