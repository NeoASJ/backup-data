# Retail Footfall & Store Analytics Intelligence

An advanced computer vision solution that transforms standard video surveillance feeds into actionable store layout insights. By tracking shopper foot traffic in real time and accumulating dynamic density maps, this platform empowers retail managers and supermarket operators to optimize floor layouts, eliminate dead zones, and dramatically boost sales per square foot.

---

## Key Business Capabilities

* **Dynamic Heatmap Generation:** Continuously tracks customer movement throughout the retail space to create high-resolution spatial density maps.
* **Real-Time & Historical Traffic Monitoring:** Distinguishes instantaneous shopper counts from cumulative footfall to understand peak hours and foot traffic trends.
* **Foot-Level Spatial Mapping:** Maps shopper trajectories based on precise ground-plane tracking rather than bounding-box centroids, delivering accurate floor-level insights.
* **Side-by-Side Live Analytics Output:** Provides an intuitive dual-panel visualization comparing raw live video feeds with real-time intelligence overlays.

---

## Retail & Supermarket Use Cases

### 1. Eliminating "Dead Zones" & Boosting Sales
* **Identify Undervisited Spots:** Discover underperforming aisles, cold zones, or hidden endcaps that shoppers consistently bypass.
* **Strategic Product Placement:** Move high-margin impulse items or promotional goods directly into high-traffic paths identified by the density map.
* **Layout Optimization:** Redesign store flow (e.g., modifying shelf spacing or display angles) to guide customer navigation through previously dead spots.

### 2. Promotional & Endcap Effectiveness
* **A/B Testing Display Layouts:** Measure the exact foot traffic generated by seasonal displays or supplier-sponsored endcaps over time.
* **Monetizing Retail Media Space:** Provide suppliers with verifiable, data-backed proof of shopper exposure for premium shelf placements.

### 3. Queue & Checkout Management
* **Bottleneck Detection:** Spot high-congestion zones near registers or popular counters to adjust staffing dynamically and prevent long wait times.
* **Staff Deployment:** Align floor associate schedules with peak traffic hours and high-volume departments.

---

## Core Features & Workflow

1. **Video Feed Processing:** Ingests standard RTSP store camera streams at optimized frame intervals for lightweight processing.
2. **Shopper Ground-Plane Localization:** Pinpoints each customer's exact standing position relative to the retail floor.
3. **Gaussian Spatial Splatting:** Accumulates spatial density into a calibrated grid using smooth Gaussian decay, accurately representing dwelling time and high-traffic corridors.
4. **Adaptive Normalization:** Dynamic visual scaling prevents brief crowding spikes from washing out historical traffic patterns.
5. **Analytics Rendering:** Exports synchronized side-by-side MP4 video reports containing embedded live operational counts and heatmap overlays.
